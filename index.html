Questions

Everything you’d ask us first.

Short answers about running Tillora in your shop. Pick a topic or search.

Does Tillora need the internet?

No. Billing, stock, reports and printing all work fully offline. The internet is only used when you choose to send something on WhatsApp.

What computer do I need?

One Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) with 4 GB of RAM or more. No server and no account to create.

How long does setup take?

About ten minutes. Pick your shop type, add your name and GSTIN, and choose the ready-made categories with their GST rates already filled in.

Can I move from another billing app?

Yes. Import your products from Excel with the ready template, and existing ChitTill data can be brought over during setup.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. Every install starts with a 14-day free trial with every feature, no card and no sign-up. Nothing you add during the trial is lost when you buy.

Is it ready for GST?

Yes. Bills show CGST, SGST or IGST with HSN codes, and you can export GSTR-1 and the month’s registers for your accountant.

Can I give a GST tax invoice to a business customer?

Yes. Add the buyer’s GSTIN at the counter and Tillora prints a B2B tax invoice, with IGST when they are in another state.

How can customers pay?

Cash with change worked out, UPI with a QR for the exact amount, card, a split across them, or pay later with the due tracked to the rupee.

How do returns and exchanges work?

Take the item back against its bill and Tillora issues a credit note. The customer can use it on their next bill, and stock goes back on the shelf.

Can I run offers like Buy 2 Get 1?

Yes. Set up Buy X get Y, percentage off or spend-and-save once, and the counter applies it on every bill by itself.

Does it handle sizes and colours?

Yes. Each product keeps its sizes and colours with the pieces left in each, and the counter shows them as a grid so you pick the right one.

Can I print barcode labels?

Yes. Print labels with the price and barcode on any label printer installed on Windows, for new stock as it comes in.

Will it tell me when stock runs low?

Yes. Low-stock alerts show what to reorder, and a reorder list by supplier can go to each supplier on WhatsApp.

Can I record purchases from suppliers?

Yes. Enter a supplier bill and the stock and costs update by themselves, so profit reports stay accurate.

Does it have loyalty points?

Yes. Customers earn points on every bill and use them on the next one. VIP, regular and at-risk groups show who to look after.

Can I send bills on WhatsApp?

Yes. Send the bill, birthday wishes, due reminders and offers from your own WhatsApp, with ready-made messages.

What is the customer screen?

A second screen facing the customer shows the bill as it builds and a UPI QR to scan. Customers trust what they can see.

Where is my data kept? Is it safe?

On your own PC, never on our servers. Tillora backs it up automatically to a folder you choose, such as a pen drive or a synced folder.

Can my staff see profits and reports?

Only if you let them. Every user signs in with their own PIN, and reports, costs and settings stay with the owner.

What if I forget my PIN?

Use the recovery key you saved during setup to get back in and set a new PIN. Your data stays as it was.

Which printers and scanners work?

Any 80 mm or 58 mm thermal printer, A4 printers and label printers installed on Windows, plus any USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner.

Does the cash drawer open by itself?

Yes. A cash drawer connected to the bill printer opens when a cash bill is saved.

How is Tillora priced?

After the trial, choose a yearly or a lifetime license. Ask us on WhatsApp from inside the app; enter the key and carry on.

Will you help me set it up?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will help you import your products, connect your printers and bill your first customer.
